## Strategic conclusion

КД «905» is a useful first real-project vertical slice because the developer page gives a coherent story that is not reducible to a generic “comfortable modern residential complex”: a small club house in the central part of Voronezh, a deliberately quiet street context, terraces, park-like car-free courtyard, underground parking, lobby/reception, and a limited stated apartment count. The landing should turn those verified points into a visitor journey, not copy the developer's page section-by-section.

Proposed narrative hypothesis (requires KEY approval before public use): **a private, quieter rhythm in the centre of the city**. This is a marketing synthesis of the developer's own “Безмятежность в центре событий” line and its description of a quiet central location; it is not a factual claim beyond the cited source.

## Facts that must not be reused without a fresh check

- The stated construction/delivery window is **Q3 2024 – Q4 2026**. As this review is dated 2026-11-05, it is time-sensitive and must be rechecked against current official documentation before any public use.
- Construction progress videos are not a substitute for a current formal construction-status source.
- The page does not establish current apartment availability, current prices, actual remaining inventory, mortgage eligibility, discounts, payment schedule or parking-space availability.
- Infrastructure names do not establish exact distances or walking times.
- Some landscaping and resident-experience details are described as planned or future management-company activity; do not present them as completed.
- The official page contains a rights restriction: materials may not be used without the developer's consent and a link to the source. Do not copy its images, renders, floor plans, video, copy blocks or other media into KEY pages until usage permission is documented.

## Landing story and visitor questions

The new page should answer the visitor's questions in this order, with each section included only if it earns its place:

1. **Is this the kind of home and environment I want?** — a club-house scale, central location and quiet-street positioning.
2. **What makes the place distinctive in real life?** — architecture, terraces, courtyard and lobby, backed by approved visuals and confirmed completion state.
3. **How does everyday life work here?** — parking, storage, access, pets/children/cycling, with exact terms confirmed rather than inferred.
4. **What kind of apartment can fit my needs?** — actual layouts, areas, floor and terrace applicability, verified and current.
5. **What should I clarify before deciding?** — finishing scope, construction status, prices, availability and purchase conditions. These should lead into a KEY consultation, not disclose the full broker-side sales process.
6. **What is my next step?** — request a tailored selection, presentation or viewing through KEY, each CTA retaining its context.
